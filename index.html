Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Countdown to Ruin (“the game”), developed by Hiraeru Studio (“we”), handles information. The short version: we do not run servers of our own, we do not show ads and we do not sell your data. If you agree, the game sends usage statistics that help us balance it, and you can change that choice at any time.

1. Information we collect

The game has no account system of its own and no advertising. We do not receive or store personal information on any server of ours.

Usage statistics (only if you agree). The first time you open the game, it asks whether you want to share usage data. If you accept, the game sends gameplay events through Google Analytics for Firebase: runs started and finished (hero, difficulty, result), missions played, trails chosen, cards picked, bought, removed and played, shop visits, talents chosen, tutorial steps seen and the game language. Firebase also records some events on its own, such as the first time the game is opened, each session and the time spent playing. Together with these events, Firebase receives an app-instance identifier (a random ID created for this installation), the device brand and model, the Android version, the device language, the app version, the store the game was installed from, and the approximate country, region and city, derived from the network address.

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, Play Games player name, advertising ID or precise location, and this data is never used for advertising. If you decline, none of it is collected. You can change your choice at any time: title screen → Settings → Usage data.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only: your progress (unlocked cards, hero mastery, difficulty levels won), the run in progress, and your settings (language, volume and your usage data choice). This data never leaves your device unless you connect to Google Play Games, as described below.

3. Google Play services

The game is distributed through Google Play and uses services provided by Google:

  • Google Play Games Services (optional). If you sign in, your game progress is saved to the Saved Games storage of your own Google account, so that it can be restored on another device or after reinstalling. The game reads your Play Games player name only to show it on the title screen. We cannot access your Google account, your e-mail address or your password.
  • Google Play purchases. The purchase of the game is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (optional). If you agreed to share usage data, Google processes the usage statistics described in section 1 on our behalf. They are sent encrypted and stored on Google servers.

Google handles the data involved in these services under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions

The game does not ask you for any permission. It uses permissions that Android grants automatically, without a prompt: vibration (touch feedback), internet access and network state for the Google services listed above, Google Play purchases, keeping the device awake for a moment while usage statistics are sent, and reading from Google Play the store the game was installed from. It does not access your contacts, location (GPS), camera, microphone, photos or files, and it does not use the advertising ID.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data and we do not share it for advertising. The usage statistics are processed by Google, as our service provider, as described above. Nobody else receives them.

6. Security

Local data stays in the private storage area that Android reserves for the game. Data saved to Google Play Games is transmitted and stored by Google using its own security measures.

7. Retention and deletion

  • In the game: title screen → Settings → Reset progress erases your progress on the device and, if you are connected, in your Google Play Games account.
  • On the device: uninstalling the game removes all local data.
  • In your Google account: you can delete the game's saved data in the Play Games app, under Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  • Usage statistics: unchecking Usage data in Settings stops the collection immediately. Data already sent is kept in Google Analytics for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Apart from these statistics, we keep nothing ourselves. The statistics are linked only to the random installation ID, which the game does not show and we cannot link to you, so we are unable to find and delete one person’s statistics on request; uninstalling the game ends that ID. If you need help deleting anything else, write to us.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to handle data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
